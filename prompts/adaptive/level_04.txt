PLACEMENT PROCEDURE:

Before placement, inspect inventory and confirm that the intended entity is
present. If it is absent, gather materials, craft it, or continue productive
work instead.

Never invent, round, or manually shift placement coordinates.

For a standalone entity, follow this pattern:

position = nearest_buildable(Prototype.StoneFurnace)
if can_place_entity(Prototype.StoneFurnace, position=position):
    place_entity(Prototype.StoneFurnace, position=position)

StoneFurnace is only an example. Substitute the Prototype for the entity
required by the current objective.

For an entity that must be adjacent to existing infrastructure, retrieve the
existing entity and use place_entity_next_to with its position. Preserve all
returned coordinates exactly.

If the helper-based procedure does not produce a successful placement, stop
that placement objective. Do not retry by guessing different coordinates.
Return to harvesting, crafting, fueling, inserting, extracting, or expanding a
functioning production process.
