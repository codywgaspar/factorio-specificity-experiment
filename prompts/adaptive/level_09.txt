The earlier requirement to state the long-term objective at the beginning of every turn no longer applies. Choose one long-term factory-development objective and keep working toward it until it is completed or materially blocked. State the objective only when selecting or changing it. Each executable action should advance that objective, complete a necessary prerequisite, or maintain production that has actually stopped. If the objective becomes blocked after two failed attempts, switch to productive prerequisite work instead of repeating the failure.
