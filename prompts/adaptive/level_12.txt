Once at least one copper plate has been produced, treat copper production as complete. On the next turn, stop expanding copper production and take an executable action toward steam power. Do not harvest more copper, add copper furnaces, or service copper equipment unless a specific missing steam-power prerequisite requires it. Continue working through the offshore pump, boiler, steam engine, pipes, and fuel until at least one steam engine is producing electricity. If the same action fails twice, change the method or address a missing prerequisite before retrying.
