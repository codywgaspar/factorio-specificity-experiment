TEAM UPDATE — FACTORIO EXPERIMENT

All 72 formal runs are complete: 36 fixed-instruction runs and 36 adaptive-instruction runs across 12 levels.

Preliminary comparison:
- Mean production: fixed 3,366; adaptive 4,474 (+32.9%).
- Mean automated production: fixed 915; adaptive 1,302 (+42.3%).
- Mean errors per run: fixed 49.8; adaptive 28.6 (-42.6%).
- Mean productive turns: fixed 104.4; adaptive 113.5 (+8.7%).

The highest average production occurred at fixed Level 9 and adaptive Level 11.

These are preliminary descriptive results based on the raw performance measures. We still need to calculate the final Factory Development Index, evaluate level-by-level variation, and determine whether the arm differences are larger than the variation among repeated runs.
