Build the highest-performing automated factory possible within the available interaction budget.

Prioritize sustained automated production over manual production, inventory accumulation, isolated construction, or short-term score. Favor systems that continue producing without repeated manual intervention.

Treat the factory as an integrated system consisting of resource extraction, material processing, power generation, logistics, research, and manufacturing. Consider how changes to one subsystem affect the others.

Use the following general progression when appropriate: establish extraction and smelting, secure reliable fuel, generate electrical power, produce science packs, conduct research, unlock electronics and assembly, and then expand into more advanced production. Depart from this progression only when the observed factory state provides a clear reason to do so.
