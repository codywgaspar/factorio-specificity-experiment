ADAPTIVE REVIEW: RECOVERED LEVEL 7 TO LEVEL 8

What improved:
Mean production increased approximately 10%, and mean automated production
increased approximately 5%. Average errors decreased approximately 31%, and
the longest error streak decreased from 7.7 to 2.7 turns. The simplified
failure rule continued to control extended error loops.

What did not improve:
Production per 1,000 ticks decreased approximately 8%, automated production
per 1,000 ticks decreased approximately 14%, and productive turns decreased
from 128.7 to 123. Inspection and manual harvesting both increased.

Strategic limitation:
The Level 7 prompt asked the model to select a long-term factory-development
objective, but none of the three runs clearly declared or maintained one.
No run produced copper-related items, built power infrastructure, or issued
a research command. Across the three runs, the model continued concentrating
on manual harvesting and servicing existing stone furnaces.

Options considered:
1. Assign one concrete strategic objective, such as copper processing.
2. Require the model to explicitly declare and maintain one strategy.
3. Provide a complete sequence of factory-development milestones.
4. Repeat the existing long-term strategy instruction.

Decision:
The team selected the explicit strategy declaration. This preserves the
model's autonomy while making its chosen objective visible and measurable in
the logs. Each executable action should advance the declared objective or a
required prerequisite. Objective changes should be explained.

Level 8 intervention:
At the beginning of each turn, the model must state its current long-term
factory-development objective. It should keep the same objective until it is
completed or materially blocked and connect each action to that objective.
Routine maintenance does not count unless it directly supports the strategy.
