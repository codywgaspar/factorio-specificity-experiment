ADAPTIVE REVIEW: LEVEL 11 TO LEVEL 12

Observed results:
Level 11 produced a mean production score of 6,199 and a mean automated-production score of 1,704. Compared with Level 10, production increased by approximately 26% and automated production increased by approximately 13%.

What worked:
- All three runs established copper-plate production.
- Production remained relatively consistent across runs.
- Each run continued producing through the final 50 turns.
- The concrete copper objective supported strong overall performance.

What did not work:
- No run completed the intended transition to steam power.
- Run 1 mentioned steam power at turn 68 but returned to copper and iron work.
- Run 2 mentioned steam power at turn 80 but continued expanding copper production.
- Run 3 never stated the steam-power objective.
- No run crafted or placed an offshore pump, boiler, steam engine, or pipe.
- No run confirmed electricity generation.
- Mean errors increased from approximately 10 at Level 10 to 16 at Level 11.

Interpretation:
The model understood and completed the first milestone but treated working copper production as an objective to continue optimizing. Stating the second milestone was not sufficient to cause an operational transition.

Level 12 decision:
Add an explicit completion trigger. Once one copper plate is confirmed, copper production is considered complete and the next executable action must advance steam power. Copper work is allowed only when it directly supplies a missing steam-power prerequisite.
