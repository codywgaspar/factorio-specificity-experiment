ADAPTIVE REVIEW: RECOVERED LEVEL 8 TO LEVEL 9

What improved:
- The model stated an objective on 435 of 450 turns, or 96.7%.
- Manual harvesting decreased by approximately 36%.
- Production per 1,000 ticks increased by approximately 8%.
- Automated production per 1,000 ticks increased by approximately 7%.

What did not improve:
- Mean production decreased from 5,311 to 3,347, a 37% decline.
- Mean automated production decreased from 1,594 to 943, a 41% decline.
- Mean errors increased from 11.7 to 26.7, a 129% increase.
- Productive turns decreased from 123.0 to 114.3.
- The model frequently restated or rephrased its objective without developing
  copper processing, power, research, logistics, or manufacturing.
- One run became caught in repeated wooden-chest lookup and retrieval errors.

Interpretation:
The model complied with the requirement to declare an objective, but the
declarations did not produce stable long-term strategy. Repeating the objective
appears to have consumed attention without improving strategic execution.

Options considered:
1. Assign one concrete objective.
2. Require an objective to create a new capability.
3. Provide a fixed milestone sequence.
4. Remove the per-turn declaration requirement while retaining long-term
   strategic direction.

Decision:
Option 4 was selected. Level 9 removes repetitive objective declarations but
continues to require one persistent factory-development objective. The purpose
is to determine whether less narration allows more attention to execution while
preserving strategic continuity.

Level 9 success indicators:
- Higher production and automated production than Level 8.
- Fewer errors and repeated-action streaks.
- A more stable objective across turns.
- Evidence of at least one new factory capability.
- Less routine inspection or maintenance that does not advance the objective.
