ADAPTIVE REVIEW: RECOVERED LEVEL 4 TO LEVEL 5

Level 4 results:
- Mean production score: 4,983.33
- Mean automated production score: 1,642.67
- Mean errors: 11.67
- Mean longest error streak: 3.00
- Mean productive turns: 120.67
- Mean productive-turn rate: 80.4%

Change from recovered Level 3:
- Production remained essentially stable, decreasing 0.7%.
- Automated production increased 6.7%.
- Errors decreased 75.7%.
- Longest error streak decreased 90.4%.
- Productive turns increased 33.1%.
- Production and automation became substantially more consistent.

What worked:
- Placement errors fell from 84 at Level 3 to 3 at Level 4.
- Runs 1 and 2 remained productive during 86% of their turns.
- All three runs avoided long consecutive error loops.
- The model generally preserved and serviced functioning production.

What did not work:
- The model made no recorded calls to nearest_buildable,
  can_place_entity, or place_entity_next_to.
- Inventory errors represented 22 of 35 total errors.
- The model frequently inspected state without performing the action described
  in its plan.
- Run 3 repeated essentially the same inspection pattern for 38 turns.
- During the end of Run 3, the model repeatedly stated that it would insert
  iron ore but called only inspection functions.
- The final eight turns of Run 3 produced no additional score.

Root-cause interpretation:
The dominant problem has shifted from placement failure to execution
follow-through. The model often identifies the correct next action but does not
include the corresponding state-changing API call in its program.

Level 5 decision:
Require direct alignment between the stated plan and executable code. Limit
repeated inspection and require the model to change objectives after two
consecutive turns without meaningful state progress. Keep the underlying tool
context unchanged to preserve comparability with previous experimental levels.
