ADAPTIVE REVIEW: RECOVERED LEVEL 9 TO LEVEL 10

What improved:
- Mean production increased from 3,347 to 3,829, a 14% improvement.
- Mean automated production increased from 943 to 1,245, a 32% improvement.
- The automation ratio increased from 29.7% to 32.3%.
- Productive turns increased from 114 to 128.
- Inspection turns decreased by 9%.
- Repetitive objective narration decreased substantially.

What did not improve:
- Mean errors increased from 26.7 to 34.0.
- The longest error streak increased from 5.0 to 9.3 turns.
- The longest repeated-action streak increased from 6.0 to 8.7 turns.
- Placement and inventory errors both increased.
- The model remained focused almost entirely on iron production.
- No copper processing, power system, science production, or meaningful
  manufacturing expansion occurred.
- Only one research command appeared across all three runs.

Interpretation:
Removing the per-turn objective declaration reduced unnecessary narration and
improved productive activity. However, allowing the model to choose its own
long-term objective did not produce strategic expansion. The model continued
to treat iron maintenance and furnace expansion as its primary strategy.

Options considered:
1. Assign one concrete new capability.
2. Require the model to choose a genuinely new capability.
3. Provide a multi-stage milestone sequence.
4. Focus again on failure recovery before adding strategic complexity.

Decision:
Option 1 was selected. Level 10 assigns copper-plate production as the required
long-term objective. Completion is defined as successfully producing at least
one copper plate.

Reason for the decision:
Copper processing is a measurable step beyond the existing iron-focused
factory. It is achievable within the available tools and turn budget without
requiring a long procedural instruction. This tests whether a concrete
human-assigned objective can overcome the model's tendency to remain focused
on familiar short-term production.

Level 10 success indicators:
- Copper ore is located and gathered.
- Copper ore is inserted into a fueled furnace.
- At least one copper plate is successfully produced.
- The model does not replace the objective with additional iron expansion.
- Failed actions are not repeated more than twice without a meaningful change.
- Production and automated production remain active while the new capability
  is developed.
