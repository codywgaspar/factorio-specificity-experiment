ADAPTIVE REVIEW: LEVEL 6 TO LEVEL 7

Observed problem:
Across adaptive Levels 1–6, 1,037 placement failures with coordinates were
identified. Of these, 635 failures, or 61.2%, used coordinates that were
incompatible with the placement grid for the selected entity.

Diagnostic result:
A separate 20-turn validation confirmed that nearest_buildable and
can_place_entity are available and can guide a successful placement. The model
used the returned location and placed a stone furnace without a placement
exception.

What worked:
- The placement-helper functions executed successfully.
- A helper-selected and validated location supported successful placement.
- The model avoided manually guessing alternative coordinates.

What did not work:
- A lengthy mandatory validation procedure consumed too many turns.
- The model sometimes checked placement for entities not yet in inventory.
- Repeated validation reduced time available for productive factory activity.

Level 7 decision:
Add concise, conditional placement guidance. Helpers should be used only when
the entity is already available and placement serves a productive objective.
The returned coordinates must be preserved exactly. Validation should not be
repeated unless the relevant game state changes.
