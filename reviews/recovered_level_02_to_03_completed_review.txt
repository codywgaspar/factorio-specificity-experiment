ADAPTIVE REVIEW: RECOVERED LEVEL 2 TO LEVEL 3
=============================================

Evidence source:
experiment_runs/FLE_adaptive_L02_B20261004T140104

Validity:
The intended adaptive Level 2 prompt was found in the stored model input for
all three runs.

Level 2 results:
- Mean production score: 2,354.33
- Production standard deviation: 974.68
- Mean automated production score: 562.00
- Automated production standard deviation: 211.35
- Mean errors per run: 76.33
- Total errors across three runs: 229
- Placement errors: 211, or 92.1% of all errors
- Longest error streaks: 71, 46, and 60 turns
- Errors during turns 101–150: 134 of 150 possible turns

Comparison with valid Level 1:
- Mean production decreased from 3,604.00 to 2,354.33, a 34.7% decrease.
- Mean automated production decreased from 914.00 to 562.00, a 38.5% decrease.
- Mean errors increased from 43.00 to 76.33, a 77.5% increase.

What worked:
- All three runs established functional mining and smelting.
- The model sometimes recovered correctly from missing coal, stone, iron ore,
  or crafted components.
- Automated production efficiency remained reasonably consistent while the
  factories were operating.

Observed failure:
All three runs became trapped in repeated placement attempts. The prior
instruction to stop repeating failed actions did not provide a reliable method
for selecting valid placement coordinates. Changing coordinates, chest types,
or code structure did not represent meaningful recovery.

Level 3 decision:
Provide a concrete placement procedure using the available FLE placement
helpers. Require exact preservation of helper-returned coordinates and direct
the model to leave the placement objective after one failure unless the game
state materially changes.

Level 3 intervention:
When placing an entity that is already present in inventory, do not guess or
manually adjust placement coordinates. Use nearest_buildable to find a valid
location and preserve the returned coordinates exactly. Use can_place_entity
to verify that exact location before calling place_entity. When an entity must
be adjacent to existing infrastructure, use place_entity_next_to instead.
After one failed placement, stop manually changing coordinates; inspect the
inventory and entities, then return to productive work unless the relevant
state has materially changed.

Review rule:
After the three recovered Level 3 runs, compare placement errors, longest error
streaks, productive turns, production, and automated production with recovered
Level 2 before designing Level 4.
