# Adaptive Level 5 Completed Review

## Results

- Mean production score: 3,085, up 112% from Level 4.
- Mean automated production score: 884, up 170%.
- Total errors: 191, down 33%.
- Error recovery rate: 27.7%, compared with 6.6% at Level 4.
- Productive turns: 293 of 450, or 65.1%.
- Mean longest error streak decreased from 74.7 to 29.3 turns.
- Mean repeated-action streak decreased from 14.7 to 10.0 turns.
- Production ranged from 1,733 to 5,026 across the three runs.

## What Worked

- Simplifying the instructions restored production and automation.
- Overall errors decreased and recovery improved substantially.
- Late-run production increased from 277 points at Level 4 to 1,812 points at Level 5.
- Run 2 remained productive through all three phases.
- Run 2 made only five placement calls and achieved the highest production and automation scores.

## What Did Not Work

- Placement remained the leading error category in two of three runs.
- Run 1 entered a 53-turn error streak and produced nothing during its final 50 turns.
- The model continued attempting iron-chest placement at different coordinates.
- The improvement was inconsistent, with production ranging from 1,733 to 5,026.

## Level 6 Decision

Placement fixation remained present in two of three runs, meeting the predetermined threshold for testing a temporary chest-placement prohibition. Level 6 will test whether removing this failure attractor improves consistency without preventing meaningful factory development.
