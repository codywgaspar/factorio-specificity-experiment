ADAPTIVE REVIEW: RECOVERED LEVEL 3 TO LEVEL 4

Level 3 results:
- Mean production score: 5,020
- Mean automated production score: 1,539
- Mean errors: 48
- Mean longest error streak: 31.33
- Mean productive turns: 90.67

Change from recovered Level 2:
- Production increased 113.2%.
- Automated production increased 173.8%.
- Errors decreased 37.1%.
- Longest error streak decreased 46.9%.

What worked:
- Two runs maintained strong production by harvesting resources and servicing
  working furnaces.
- One run completed without any placement errors.
- Overall production and automation were the strongest adaptive results so far.

What went wrong:
- Placement behavior remained inconsistent.
- Two runs entered chest-placement loops.
- One run produced zero score during its final 50 turns.
- The model made no recorded calls to nearest_buildable,
  can_place_entity, or place_entity_next_to, despite those tools being named
  in the Level 3 instruction.

Root-cause interpretation:
Naming the placement helpers was insufficient. The model needs short,
executable syntax showing how the helpers connect to place_entity. Manual
coordinate guessing remains the primary cause of placement deadlocks.

Level 4 decision:
Provide an explicit helper-based placement procedure. Require exact preservation
of helper-returned coordinates and abandonment of the objective if the procedure
fails. Retain productive fallback actions so placement failure does not stop the
factory.
